# Chapter 1: The Fractured Spire

@pov: Aeloria-Vael
@location: High-Sanctuary
@faction: Order-of-the-Silver-Dawn

Dawn broke over the floating parapets of [[High-Sanctuary]] in shards of cold amber.

Aeloria stood upon the western battlement, her fingers lightly grazing the hilt of her crystalline greatsword. Below the levitating citadel, endless clouds stretched toward the horizon like an unmapped ocean.

"The resonance currents are trembling again," Archmage [[Archmage-Theron]] said, stepping quietly to her side. His robes billowed in the alpine breeze.

"The seals in the [[Whispering-Vale]]?" Aeloria asked, turning her gaze south.

"Indeed," Theron nodded grimly. "Lord [[Lord-Kaelen]] reports tremors along the deep faultlines. The [[Order-of-the-Silver-Dawn]] must prepare for the awakening."
