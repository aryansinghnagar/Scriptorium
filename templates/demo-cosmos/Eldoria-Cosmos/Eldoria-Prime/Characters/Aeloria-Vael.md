# Aeloria Vael

Aeloria Vael is the foremost swordmaster of the [[Order-of-the-Silver-Dawn]]. Trained in [[Aether-Weaving]] from childhood, she wields the crystalline greatsword *Dawnstrider*.

## Personality & Motivation
Disciplined, observant, and fiercely loyal to the protection of [[High-Sanctuary]].
