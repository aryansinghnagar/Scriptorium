# Chapter 4: The Final Reckoning

@pov: Protagonist
@location: The High Spire
@status: Draft
@tag: Climax

The summit of the high tower was open to the violent storm. Lightning tore through the bruised clouds, illuminating the figure standing at the center of the summoning circle.

Kaelen unclasped the ancient seal from his belt. There was no hatred in his chest now, only the clarity that had eluded him since the night the archive gates had splintered. He stepped into the storm.

"It ends where it began," he said, the words cutting through the roar of thunder.
