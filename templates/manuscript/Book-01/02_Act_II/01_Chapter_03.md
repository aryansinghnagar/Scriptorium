# Chapter 3: The Sanctuary of Whispers

@pov: Protagonist
@location: Mountain Fortress
@status: Draft
@tag: Midpoint Shift

The mountain pass had narrowed to a ledge no wider than a cart axle. Wind howled through the granite gorges, stinging Kaelen's face with frozen grit.

Ahead stood the gates of the sanctuary, carved directly into the living stone of the mountain. For the first time in three weeks of relentless running, Kaelen stopped retreating. He turned to face the vast expanse of the lowlands stretched out beneath the clouds.

"We cannot outrun them indefinitely," he said to the companions who had joined them along the road. "We must forge the defense here."
