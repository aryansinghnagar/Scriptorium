# Chapter 1: The Ash and the Ink

@pov: Protagonist
@focus: Capital City
@status: Draft
@tag: Inciting Incident

The bell of the Seventh Spire tolled three times, its deep bronze resonance vibrating through the timber beams of the old archives. Outside the leaded glass windows, rain fell in silver sheets over the slate rooftops of the lower ward.

Kaelen dipped his quill into the dark iron gall ink, his fingers stained purple from three days of continuous copying. On the vellum before him lay the fragments of the Old Imperial Treaty—words written three centuries ago by men who had long turned to dust.

"You should not be reading those passages, boy," Master Vance's voice broke the rhythm of the rain. The old archivist stood by the threshold, a sputtering tallow candle trembling in his gnarled hand.

"They aren't just treaties, Master," Kaelen replied quietly without lifting the nib from the page. "They are warnings."

Vance stepped forward, the floorboards groaning under his weight. His face was drawn tight, shadows pooling beneath his hollow cheeks. "Some truths are buried because the world cannot bear their weight. Close the folio. Pack your things."

Before Kaelen could ask why, a sudden crash echoed from the street below—the splintering sound of heavy oak gates giving way under iron hammers.
