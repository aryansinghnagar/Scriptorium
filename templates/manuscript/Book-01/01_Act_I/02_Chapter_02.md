# Chapter 2: The Crossing

@pov: Protagonist
@location: River Crossing
@status: Draft
@tag: Crossing Threshold

The river was a swollen black ribbon beneath the midnight fog. Behind them, the spires of the city burned with a pale, unnatural fire, smoke blotting out the constellations.

"Keep your head down," Vance whispered, tossing a rough wool cloak over Kaelen's shoulders. The ferryman took the silver coin without a word, pushing the heavy wooden barge into the current.

Kaelen clutched the leather cylinder to his chest. Inside was the single scroll Vance had risked his life to extract before the archives fell. Every instinct screamed at Kaelen to turn back, to return to the only life he had ever known. But as the dark shoreline receded into the mist, he knew that world was gone forever.
