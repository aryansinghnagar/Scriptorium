## 3. Structural Milestone Outline (Three-Act / Hero's Journey)

### 🎬 ACT I: The Ordinary World & The Call (0% – 25%)
- **01. Hook & Ordinary World (0% - 10%)**:
  - Introduce protagonist, their current life, flaw, and longing.
  - *Target Scene*: `Book-01/01_Act_I/01_Chapter_01.md`
- **02. Inciting Incident (12%)**:
  - The catastrophic disruption that shatters normal life.
- **03. Refusal of the Call & Debate (15% - 20%)**:
  - Reluctance, fear of the unknown, weighing the stakes.
- **04. Crossing the First Threshold (25%)**:
  - Point of No Return. The protagonist steps into the special world.
  - *Target Scene*: `Book-01/01_Act_I/02_Chapter_02.md`

---

### ⚡ ACT II: Trials, Allies, Midpoint & Crisis (25% – 75%)
- **05. Tests, Enemies, and New Alliances (30% - 40%)**:
  - Learning the new rules, meeting side characters, early skirmishes.
- **06. The Midpoint Shift (50%)**:
  - Active turn. Protagonist stops reacting and goes on the offensive; stakes become personal.
  - *Target Scene*: `Book-01/02_Act_II/01_Chapter_03.md`
- **07. The Bad Guys Close In & False Victory (60% - 70%)**:
  - Pressure escalates, plans fail, internal fractures emerge.
- **08. The All-Is-Lost / Dark Night of the Soul (75%)**:
  - Total apparent defeat. The mentor falls, the plan is ruined, the Lie is exposed.

---

### 🏆 ACT III: Climax, Transformation & Resolution (75% – 100%)
- **09. The Epiphany / True Need Discovered (80% - 85%)**:
  - Protagonist finally sheds their Lie and synthesizes their true Need.
- **10. The Climax & Final Showdown (90%)**:
  - Ultimate confrontation with the antagonist.
  - *Target Scene*: `Book-01/03_Act_III/01_Chapter_04.md`
- **11. Resolution & The New Normal (95% - 100%)**:
  - The dust settles. The world is irrevocably altered.
